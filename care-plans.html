Quick answers

Is this the same as my Wix bill?

No. Your Wix plan pays Wix to host your site. A care plan pays for our time to keep it updated and working. They're billed separately.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. Your plan runs to the end of that month. Plans are billed monthly in advance.

Can I move my site somewhere else?

Yes. Your site and domain are in your name, so you can take them anywhere, anytime.

Do I need a care plan?

No, it's optional. It's for owners who would rather hand the website off and focus on the business.
