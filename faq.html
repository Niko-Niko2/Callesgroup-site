Web design FAQ

Answers about payment, ownership, timelines and care plans. Still unsure? See our pricing or get a free quote.

How does payment work?

50% deposit to start and the rest before your site goes live. For projects over $2,500, it's 50% to start, 25% when you approve the design, and 25% before launch. We accept card through a Wix invoice, Zelle, or check. Invoices are due within 7 days.

Is the deposit refundable?

The deposit is non-refundable once work starts. Work starts at our kickoff call or when we send your first design, whichever comes first.

Who owns the website?

You do. Your Wix account and domain are set up in your name and you pay for them directly. We just need collaborator access to build. Your logo files and full admin access are handed over once your final payment is made.

What counts as a round of changes?

One round is one list of all your feedback, sent together within 5 business days. Extra rounds are $75 per hour.

How long does it take?

About 2 to 5 weeks depending on the package. The clock starts once we have your text, photos and logo.

What if I need to pause?

No problem. If a project goes quiet for 30 days, we pause it. The next payment comes due, and restarting costs $150.

Can I cancel a care plan?

Yes, anytime. Your plan runs to the end of that month. Plans are billed monthly in advance.

Do you work outside Elgin?

Yes. We work across the Fox Valley, the Chicago suburbs and Chicago, in person or over video.

Can you show my project in your portfolio?

We may show finished work in our portfolio. Just tell us if you'd prefer we didn't.
