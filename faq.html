Payment

A 50% deposit starts the project and the rest is due before launch. Projects over $2,500 split into 50% to start, 25% at design approval and 25% before launch. Pay by Zelle or cash. Card payments are coming soon. Invoices are due in 7 days.

The deposit is non-refundable once work starts. Work starts at the kickoff call or the first design, whichever comes first.

Need something that's not here? Tell us on your quote call and we'll send you a clear, flat quote.

Ownership

Yes. Your Wix account and domain are in your name, and you pay Wix for them directly. Your logo files and full admin access are handed over after the final payment.

Yes. Your Wix plan and domain are billed by Wix to you, in your name. Our prices cover the design and build, and care plans cover upkeep.

Finished work may be shown in our portfolio unless you tell us you'd rather opt out.

Timeline

1 to 4 weeks depending on the package: 1 to 2 weeks for Launch, 2 to 3 weeks for Growth and 3 to 4 weeks for Premium. The clock starts once we have your text, photos and logo.

One list of all your feedback, sent within 5 business days. Launch includes 2 rounds, Growth and Premium include 3. Extra rounds are $100 per hour.

After 30 days with no reply, the project pauses and the next payment comes due. Restarting costs $150.
