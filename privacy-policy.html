Privacy policy

Last updated November 4, 2026. This page explains what information Calles Group collects through callesgroup.com, how we use it and the choices you have.

Who we are

Calles Group is a website design and branding business based in Elgin, Illinois. When this policy says "we" or "us," it means Calles Group. You can reach us at andrew@callesgroup.com.

What we collect

When you fill out our free quote form, we collect what you type in:

  • Your name and business name
  • Your email address and phone number
  • The services you're interested in, your budget range and your message

If you email or call us, we keep that conversation and the contact details that come with it. Like most websites, our hosting provider also records basic technical information when you visit, such as your browser type, device, the pages you view and your approximate location based on your IP address.

How we use it

  • To reply to your quote request and talk with you about your project
  • To send proposals, invoices and project updates if you become a client
  • To keep our website secure, working properly and free of spam
  • To understand, in general terms, how people use our site so we can improve it

We don't sell your information, and we don't add you to a marketing email list unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

Our website and quote form run on Wix. Wix stores form submissions and site data for us as our service provider. You can read Wix's privacy policy to learn how they handle data. We also use standard tools to send email and invoices. Otherwise, we only share your information if the law requires it.

Cookies

Our site uses cookies and similar technologies that Wix needs to run the site, keep forms secure and measure basic site traffic. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the site, like the quote form, may not work without them.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests and project records for as long as we need them to work with you and to meet tax and business record-keeping requirements. If you never become a client, you can ask us to delete your request at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we have about you. Just email andrew@callesgroup.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Children

Our services are for businesses, and our site isn't meant for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we'll post the new version on this page and change the date at the top.
