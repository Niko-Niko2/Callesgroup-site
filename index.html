Common questions

Who owns the website?

You do. Your Wix account and domain are set up in your name and you pay for them directly. We just need collaborator access to build. Your logo files and full admin access are handed over once your final payment is made.

How long does it take?

About 2 to 5 weeks depending on the package. The clock starts once we have your text, photos and logo.

How does payment work?

50% deposit to start and the rest before your site goes live. For projects over $2,500, it's 50% to start, 25% when you approve the design, and 25% before launch. We accept card through a Wix invoice, Zelle, or check. Invoices are due within 7 days.
